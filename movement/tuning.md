# Tuning

The standard every external deliverable is held to. The one who writes and the
one who reviews tune to the same pitch.

## The Golden Standard: the Nodding Reader

The target reader is a colleague from a neighboring subfield, smart but not
initiated. The draft opens with the big story, breaks it into smaller stories,
and tackles them one by one. Transitions are so natural that there is no place
to raise a question; everything makes so much sense that the reader keeps
nodding.

The goal is a coherent story, not a complete record of truth.

## Known Failure Modes

- **Zigzag**: a topic discussed, dropped in an abrupt shift, then resumed. Each
  topic is handled once, in full, in its place; once left, never reopened.
- **Distraction**: content that does not serve the narrative. Removed, not kept
  for completeness.
- **Redefinition**: a term defined again after first use. Terms are defined
  once; thereafter the term stands alone.
- **Undefined insider terms**: jargon the reader cannot know; defined or
  replaced.
- **Dashes**: an em dash in any form; the sentence is carrying two thoughts.
  Split it, or use a comma or colon.
- **Disproportion**: length out of proportion to a point's role in the story.
