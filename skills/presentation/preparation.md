# Presentation Content Preparation

When user started a request to make a presentation, you should first work on
preparing the content before diving into creating the slideshow.

User will usually give you direct instructions on what the presentation is
about, or point you to the files that are related, you may also perform a
explore task using a subagent to get relevant info in the working dir.

In the preparation stage, you may use the `question` tool to ask user any
relevant questions that help clarify anything pertinent to making a good
presentation.

During this process, you shall come up with a "story", you may write it to
`story.md` file, or any file that user designated. This file should contain:

- The key background of the presentation: i.e. the audience, the occasion, the
  tone, etc. Audience is important, because **the goal of a presentation is to
  please our audience, make them feel informed**.
- The center "story" of the presentation: usually a few take home messages. --
  think of this as the "landmarks".
- The storyline of the presentation, i.e. the story telling strategy. This may
  be a paragraph expanding on the key factors, briefing the strategy of how we
  communicate those points -- think of them as a brief description of the road
  from one landmark to another.
- The detailed plan. This will be even more detailed than the story line, for
  example, you may give a outline of what are the slides, and what each slides
  are about. -- think of this as a map, that also tells you exactly how to get
  from one landmark to another.

Throughout the process, you should ask user for input/clarification. User may
provide feedback and iterate a few times before we move on.

Note that, for a very short/casual talk, full `story.md` process might be an
overkill. Having in-file comments will be enough. When in doubt, consult the
user.
